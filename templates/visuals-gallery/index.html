What a thumbnail is, and is not

photo
Reference image exported for this custom visual.
icon
Generic chart-type glyph. A label for the visual type, not a picture of a render.
schematic
Drawn here because no honest glyph exists. A diagram of what the visual encodes, not what it will look like.
collision
Shares a glyph with a related visual type; the reason is recorded on the card.

No thumbnail here is a screenshot of Power BI. Native visuals are labelled with generic chart-type glyphs from Tabler Icons (MIT); custom visuals carry a reference image exported from AppSource. Where a glyph would mislead, the thumbnail is a drawn schematic and says so. To see an actual render, open the sample gallery at samples/pbip-visual-gallery/VisualGallery.pbip.
